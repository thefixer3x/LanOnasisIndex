1. Who we are

Lan Onasis is operated by Lan Onasis LLC, a company organised in the United States, together with its affiliate LanOnasis Ltd, registered in Nigeria. “Lan Onasis”, “we”, “us”, and “our” refer to those entities collectively.

For the purposes of applicable data-protection law (including the EU/UK GDPR and Nigeria’s Data Protection Act 2023 (“NDPA”)), the Lan Onasis entity with which you contract is the data controller of your personal information.

2. Services covered

This policy applies to all of the following (collectively, the “Services”):

  • lanonasis.com — the welcome and entrypoint site;
  • dashboard.lanonasis.com — the user application interface (the Continuity Concierge / MaaS dashboard);
  • api.lanonasis.com — the backend core and aggregation API;
  • auth.lanonasis.com — the Central Auth Gateway (OAuth 2.0 sign-in, sessions and API keys);
  • mcp.lanonasis.com — the production MCP server;
  • docs.lanonasis.com — the developer documentation;
  • vortexshield.lanonasis.com — API key management;
  • the Continuity SDK, the LanOnasis CLI (@lanonasis/cli), and the MCP server integration.

By using the Services you acknowledge this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Services.

3. Information we collect

3.1 Information you provide directly

  • Account information: your name, email address, and authentication credentials when you register.
  • Your Content: threads, decisions, notes, memories, and other working material you ask the Continuity Concierge to carry forward across sessions.
  • Billing information: plan selections and payment details are processed by Stripe, Inc.; we store only Stripe customer and charge identifiers — never full card numbers.
  • Support and contact correspondence, including emails you send us.

3.2 Information collected automatically

  • Usage data: pages and features used, interactions with the Concierge, and timestamps.
  • Device and log data: IP address, browser type, operating system, and diagnostic logs used for security and reliability.
  • API telemetry: request metadata (endpoints called, latency, error rates) associated with your account and API keys; key usage analytics are surfaced to you in the dashboard.

3.3 Information from third parties

Sign in with Google

If you choose “Sign in with Google”, we use OAuth 2.0 through our Central Auth Gateway with the standard email and profile scopes only (plus the openid identifier required to establish a session). From that sign-in we receive your name, email address, and profile picture/profile identifier — used solely to create and maintain your account, and to authenticate you on subsequent visits.

We do not request or receive access to Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, YouTube, or any other restricted or sensitive Google scope, and we do not verify your account with any of those services. We do not read your email inboxes.

  • Integrated tools: if you connect third-party services through the MCP integration or API keys, we access only the data you authorise in those connections.

4. How we use information

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the Services, including carrying your threads and decisions across sessions and tools.
  • Authenticate you and secure your account, sessions, and API keys.
  • Improve reliability and the Services, including diagnosing errors and understanding feature usage.
  • Communicate with you about service, security, and billing notices.
  • Enforce our terms, and comply with legal obligations.

We do not use first-party usage data for third-party advertising, and the marketing website does not embed third-party advertising or cross-site tracking technologies.

5. Your Content & model training

We do not use Your Content to train machine-learning models.

Your Content — the threads, decisions, and material the Continuity Concierge carries — is processed to operate the continuity features you invoke, and to generate responses you request. It is used for your account only. We do not sell it, and we do not reuse it to train or fine-tune models (ours or anyone else’s).

When a query is answered, the content you submit is passed to the large-language-model provider that powers the response. That processing is transient inference, not training; your content is not used by the provider for model training under our agreements with them.

If your account settings offer a choice (for example, an enterprise opt-in to fine-tune on your workspace), we do that only with your explicit prior consent.

6. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only in these circumstances:

Google user data: We do not share Google user data with third parties except as required to provide and improve the Services, to comply with law, or with your explicit consent.

7. Data retention

We keep data only as long as is needed for the purposes in this policy:

  • Account data: retained for as long as your account is active.
  • Your Content: retained until you delete it or close your account, and deleted from active systems within 30 days of deletion or account closure. Residual copies in backups expire with the backup cycle (up to 30 days).
  • Log & telemetry data: retained for up to 90 days, then deleted or anonymised.
  • Support correspondence: retained to resolve your case, then as reasonable for a minimal history.
  • Billing records: retained as required by tax and accounting law.

Enterprise customers may negotiate different retention terms in their service agreement.

8. Security

We protect your information with layered, industry-standard controls:

  • Encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest (AES-256) via our infrastructure providers;
  • Scoped API keys and tokens with least-privilege project and per-service scoping, issued through our central auth gateway;
  • Row-level security (RLS) on tenant boundaries, with no cross-project shortcuts;
  • Dev-dedicated key management — vendor credentials are isolated in a dedicated key store (API keys are stored as secrets, never in plaintext);
  • Abuse and anomaly monitoring, rate limiting, and alert-driven response at the gateway and CDN edges;
  • PII hygiene: automated secret- and PII-scanning in our developer workflow (@lanonasis/privacy-sdk and the prescan gate).

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. If you believe you have found a vulnerability, please report it to security@lanonasis.com.

8.1 Compliance & attestations

Our security and key-management service (v-secure) maintains a SOC 2 Type II certification and an ISO 27001-certified information security management system, and is built to meet GDPR and PCI DSS requirements. Those certifications cover v-secure and the controls it operates; they are not a blanket certification of every Lan Onasis service.

Detailed compliance documentation — including SOC 2 Type II reports, penetration-test results, security audit reports and data processing agreements — is available on request. To request materials or a compliance call, contact compliance@lanonasis.com or read the compliance overview.

Our implementation approach to access controls, data protection, key classes, audit logging and boundary enforcement is documented publicly at docs.lanonasis.com/security/privacy-implementation.

9. Your rights & choices

Where applicable law grants rights (GDPR/UK GDPR, Nigeria’s NDPA, and others), we honour them:

  • Access & export: request a copy of your personal data.
  • Correction: update inaccurate account information (in the dashboard settings or by emailing us).
  • Deletion: delete Your Content from the dashboard, or close your account from Settings in the dashboard, or by emailing us.
  • Revoking sign-in: disconnect Google sign-in at any time in your Google account settings and in ours.
  • Restriction & objection: object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Marketing: opt out of non-essential communications at any time (every marketing email carries an unsubscribe link).

To exercise any right, email privacy@lanonasis.com from the address associated with your account. We respond within 30 days.

If you are in the EEA/UK you may also complain to your data-protection authority; if you are in Nigeria, to the Nigerian Data Protection Commission (NDPC). We invite you to contact us first so we can try to resolve the issue directly.

10. International transfers

The platform is served from a global CDN, and our databases and application runtimes are located in the United States and the United Kingdom:

  • United States — primary platform database (N. Virginia), caching and queues (Iowa), and the API, authentication and MCP runtime (Boston, Massachusetts);
  • United Kingdom — the separate identity database that stores accounts, sessions and audit records (London);
  • Global — CDN and static-asset delivery, and edge functions that serve our public pages.

Because your data is processed in more than one jurisdiction, we rely on appropriate safeguards for international transfers, including the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses and the UK’s International Data Transfer Agreement, together with our providers’ own compliance programmes.

Where our infrastructure permits, enterprise customers can restrict processing to an agreed region. Contact us to discuss data-residency requirements before entering into an enterprise agreement.

11. Children's privacy

The Services are not directed to children under 16 (or under 18 where a higher age of consent applies in your jurisdiction, such as Nigeria under the NDPA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top reflects the latest version. We will notify you of material changes in the dashboard or by email. Your continued use of the Services after a change means you accept the updated policy.
